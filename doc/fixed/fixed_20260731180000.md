## ARCH-6 收尾详情

### 审计结论

| composable | ctx 参数类型 | 读取字段 | 写入字段 | 分类 |
|------------|-------------|----------|----------|------|
| useCombatLog | ICombatQuery | character.{attributes, effectiveStats, hp, maxHp} | — | 纯读取 |
| useCombatState | ICombatContext | enemy.getEnemyById | enemy.deleteEnemy | 需写入 |
| usePlayerAction | ICombatContext | character.{name, attributes, effectiveStats}, enemy.getEnemyById | enemy.takeDamage, character.takeDamage | 需写入 |
| usePlayerSkill | ICombatContext | skill.getSkill, character.{name, attributes}, enemy.getEnemyById | skill.castSkill, enemy.takeDamage, character.takeDamage | 需写入 |
| usePlayerItem | ICombatContext | inventory.getItemInfo, character.{name, attributes}, enemy.getEnemyById | enemy.takeDamage, character.takeDamage, inventory.useItem | 需写入 |
| useLootHandler | ICombatContext | inventory.getItemInfo | inventory.addItem, log.addLogEntry | 需写入 |
| usePassiveSkills | ICombatContext | character.{classId, name, hp, maxHp, mana, maxMana} | character.receiveHeal | 需写入 |
| useInitiative | ICombatContext | character.{effectiveStats, name, hp}, enemy.getEnemyById | character.{takeDamage, receiveHeal}, enemy.{takeDamage, tickCooldowns}, skill.tickCooldowns | 需写入 |
| useEnemyAction | ICombatContext | character.{name, attributes, hp, maxHp}, enemy.{calculateDamage, getAvailableSkills, getEnemyById}, skill.getSkill | character.takeDamage, enemy.{takeDamage, useSkill} | 需写入 |
| store.ts | ICombatContext | character.{name, classId} | character.{gainExp, gainGold, handleDeath}, skill.resetCooldowns, quest.onEnemyKilled, log.addLogEntry | 需写入 |

### 决策

- **保留 ICombatQuery**：useCombatLog 已收窄使用，编译期保证日志层无副作用
- **保留 ICombatCommand 作为预留接口**：符合项目硬约束，供未来战斗回放系统或纯写入场景使用
- **不强行拆分其他 composable**：8 个需写入的 composable 均同时需要读取，强行拆分会引入无意义的双 ctx 参数

### 代码修改

- [src/modules/combat/combatContext.ts](file:///d:/openSource/wow_dnd/src/modules/combat/combatContext.ts)：
  - ICombatCommand 注释（第 77-90 行）补充 ARCH-6 审计决策，明确"预留接口，当前无使用方"
  - ICombatContext 注释（第 141-151 行）补充审计结论，明确"当前所有需写入的 composable 与 store.ts 均使用 ICombatContext"
